## Metformin

**Hipotesis mecanistica:** La senal sugiere que metformin podria modular ejes relevantes de SOP como ovulacion, resistencia a la insulina, inflamacion o carga sintomatica.

**Plausibilidad clinica:** 1037 articulos coincidentes y 206 ensayos coincidentes en la base actual, con actividad reciente hasta 2026.

**Soporte reciente:**
- A randomized controlled trial comparing the efficacy of resveratrol combined with metformin versus resveratrol monotherapy in the management of polycystic ovary syndrome.
- Effects of Ziziphus jujuba, metformin, and myoinositol on pregnancy rates and metabolic parameters in infertile women with PCOS: a randomized controlled trial.
- Polymeric oral fast dissolving films of metformin: fabrication, optimization, in vitro, and in vivo evaluation.

**Nivel de evidencia actual:** moderado.
